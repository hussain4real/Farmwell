# Investor Guide

Applies through: Phase 5 - Investor Agreements and Approvals  
Last reviewed: 2026-06-11  
Audience: Investors with Farmwell portal access  
Related app areas: Investor Portal

Use this guide when you need to review your assigned farm agreement, approved fund usage, approved evidence, funding progress, approval requests, and comments.

## What You Can See

Your portal is scoped to your own active or completed investment agreements.

You may see:

- Your assigned agreement details.
- Approved funding phase progress.
- Approved expense-level fund usage.
- Approved receipt, activity, or transfer evidence.
- Approved activity and plan-change summaries.
- Approval requests assigned to you.
- Your own comments and questions on supported records.

You should not see internal farm notes, draft records, unapproved records, unrelated farms, unrelated investors, sensitive bank details, or records outside your agreement.

## Portal Access

Investor users have investor portal access only by default.

- Use your account controls for profile and security settings.
- Use the Investor Portal for agreement and approved update review.
- Contact the farm owner or admin if you believe an agreement or approved record is missing.

## Agreements

An agreement defines your visibility boundary.

Agreement details may include:

- Farm and optional production cycle.
- Committed and funded amounts.
- Currency.
- Capital-first recovery rule.
- Profit split terms.
- Public notes and role responsibilities.
- Agreement documents approved for your access.
- Agreement status.

Inactive or unrelated agreements should not appear in your portal.

## Approved Expenses and Evidence

Approved expense-level transparency means you see finance records that have been reviewed and approved for your agreement.

Approved expense items may include:

- Category.
- Amount and currency.
- Status.
- Approved safe summary.
- Approved receipt or photo evidence.
- Related phase balance or completion status when available.

Receipt and evidence links are private Farmwell links. Do not expect direct storage paths.

## Funding Progress

Funding phase information helps you understand staged funding.

You may see:

- Requested, approved, expected, or externally released amounts when approved for your agreement.
- Completion status.
- Spending and remaining balance where available.
- Related approved expense activity.

Farmwell records external transfer tracking only. It does not collect funds, hold balances, provide escrow or wallet services, or disburse funds.

## Approval Decisions

Some records may require your decision.

You can:

- Approve.
- Reject.
- Request clarification.
- Add comments explaining the decision.

Approval decisions may apply to funding releases, threshold-triggered expenses, investor-required categories, material budget overruns, or major plan changes.

If you request clarification, the record should not be treated as approved until the question is resolved and a later decision is recorded.

## Comments and Questions

You may comment or ask questions on supported approved records.

Use comments to:

- Ask for clarification on an approved update.
- Ask about an approved expense or receipt.
- Ask about milestone or funding phase completion.
- Preserve discussion in the agreement record.

Do not use comments for legal, tax, or investment advice requests. Farmwell is an operating-record platform, not a professional advisory service.

## Current Limits

The investor portal does not currently provide harvest/output, sales proceeds, capital recovery, profit distribution, AI summaries, formal reports, or export workflows. Those are later MVP phases.
