# Farm Operations Guide

Applies through: Phase 5 - Investor Agreements and Approvals  
Last reviewed: 2026-06-11  
Audience: Farm managers, farmers, and operations/log keepers  
Related app areas: Dashboard, Farms, Field Diary, Tasks, WhatsApp Intake

Use this guide when you record farm work, keep operational history clean, upload evidence, track task progress, and convert field updates into official records.

## Responsibilities

- Record farm activity close to when work happens.
- Attach evidence to the exact activity it supports.
- Keep task statuses current.
- Give delay, blocked, skipped, or cancelled reasons when required.
- Review WhatsApp-style updates before they become official records.
- Separate internal notes from investor-safe summaries.

## Dashboard

Use Dashboard to check high-level operating status.

- Review summary counts for farms, cycles, activities, tasks, and intake.
- Use the dedicated workspaces to create or update records.
- Follow up on pending intake, open tasks, and missing evidence from the relevant page.

## Farms Workspace

Use Farms to understand the operating structure.

- Farms are the top-level operating records.
- Production units are fields, plots, pens, ponds, houses, or other farm-specific areas.
- Commodities describe what is being grown or produced.
- Production cycles describe a season or operating period.
- Plan changes record why the plan changed and what the expected impact is.

If you are not responsible for farm setup, ask an Owner or Admin to create missing farms, units, commodities, or cycles before logging activity.

## Field Diary

Use Field Diary for official activity records.

Each activity should include the best available details:

- Activity date.
- Farm, production unit, production cycle, and commodity when known.
- Activity type and description.
- Cost when the diary needs operational cost context.
- Next activity or next action.
- Status.
- Investor-safe summary when the activity may be shared externally.

The diary cost field is operational metadata. Finance totals come from Expenses, not diary cost values.

## Activity Evidence

Evidence should support a specific activity or record.

- Upload photos, receipts, invoices, videos, or documents through the evidence workflow.
- Use clear captions where available.
- Attach evidence to the official activity after a WhatsApp intake is converted.
- Do not treat raw WhatsApp intake attachments as official evidence until the intake is reviewed and converted.

Evidence is private by default and downloaded through authorized Farmwell links.

## Task Calendar

Use Tasks to track planned and manual farm work.

- Create tasks with farm, unit, cycle, due date, and clear instructions where available.
- Mark tasks complete when the work is done.
- Use delayed, blocked, skipped, or cancelled statuses only with a reason.
- Keep next steps updated so owners and managers can understand what is pending.

Tasks help operations stay organized. They do not replace official diary entries for work that has already happened.

## WhatsApp Intake

Use WhatsApp Intake for updates that arrive as messages, forwarded notes, or field photos before they are cleaned into official records.

Pending intake is not official history yet. It should preserve the source text, source date when known, sender/importer, attachments, and review state.

When reviewing pending intake:

- Convert it when it clearly describes official farm activity.
- Map it to the correct farm, unit, cycle, commodity, date, activity type, description, cost, next action, and investor-safe summary.
- Reject it with a reason when it should not become an official activity.
- Keep messy operational conversation out of investor-facing summaries.

Converted intake creates an official activity record and copies attached evidence to that activity.

## Investor-Safe Summaries

Use investor-safe summaries for clear external updates.

Good investor-safe summaries:

- State what happened in plain language.
- Avoid private staff comments, unrelated farm details, sensitive bank details, and unverified claims.
- Explain plan changes or delays without copying internal conversations.
- Match attached evidence and official records.

Internal notes and messy source text should stay internal.

## Current Limits

This guide does not cover harvest/output, sales, capital recovery, AI summaries, or formal reports because those workflows are not current through Phase 5.
